Messaging comparison

textbee vs Campfire SMS

Both let an agent text a person and read the reply. The difference is what does the sending.

By Campfire SMS · Published October 9, 2026

textbee and Campfire SMS cover the same direction: an agent texts a person and reads the reply. With textbee, an Android phone holding your SIM sends the text from your own number, and the software is open source. Campfire SMS is hosted, so there is no phone app or gateway device to keep running.

The same direction, different plumbing

textbee's AI agent notifications page (published September 5, 2026) describes the same moment Campfire SMS is built for: "Agents run for minutes or hours and then need a human: a result to read, an approval to give, a question to answer." Its answer is to send that text from an Android phone holding your SIM. Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, and other MCP clients connect through the official @textbee/mcp server, and the agent can read replies received after its question.

Campfire SMS handles the same exchange as a hosted service. Your agent sends through the connector you installed, the text reaches your phone, and your reply goes back to the agent. You don't need a dedicated Android phone, a SIM, or a gateway app.

Choose textbee if…

  • You want texts to come from your own number. textbee sends from the SIM in your phone, so recipients see your number.
  • You want open source. textbee is MIT licensed and self-hostable, so you can audit and run the whole stack.
  • You have a spare Android phone and are happy to keep it powered on with the textbee app running.
  • Your agent texts other people. Task owners, customers, or a team, at a flat monthly price with no per-message fee.
  • You want to skip number registration. textbee says there is no number to rent and no 10DLC brand or campaign registration, because it uses your own SIM. Consent rules still apply.
  • You use n8n or Zapier. textbee documents an n8n setup with an HTTP Request node to send and a Webhook node to receive.

textbee's own honest-limits note is worth reading: one phone sends roughly 10 to 15 messages a minute, and carriers can filter bulk patterns on consumer SIMs.

Choose Campfire SMS if…

  • You want the agent you already run (Codex, Claude Code, Grok Bot, OpenCode, or Grok Build) to text you when it needs an answer or has a result.
  • You want to reply from your normal SMS inbox, on an iPhone or an Android phone, with no app to install.
  • You don't want to rent a number, host a webhook, or keep a gateway device running.
  • You don't have, or don't want to dedicate, an Android phone and SIM to sending. Campfire SMS works with an iPhone or an Android phone because you only receive and reply.

Setup is three steps: sign up at campfiresms.com, verify your phone (40 free texts, no card), and run the one-time install command from your account. Then send a test text and reply. See the setup guide for each agent.

What Campfire SMS does not do

Campfire SMS is not open source or self-hosted, and texts don't come from your personal number.

  • Local agents need a live session. Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode, Grok Build, and other local agents need the computer awake and online and the session running. Campfire SMS does not wake a stopped local session. Grok Bot is the exception: an active webhook routine can start it in the cloud when you text.
  • Replies don't grant new permissions. A text can steer the task, but the agent keeps the permissions it already had. Native approvals stay in the agent's own interface.
  • SMS isn't end-to-end encrypted. Don't text secrets, and keep sensitive detail on the computer.

Common questions

Is Campfire SMS a textbee alternative?

For an agent texting you and reading your reply, yes. textbee sends from your own Android phone and SIM and is open source. Campfire SMS is hosted and needs no phone app or gateway device.

Does textbee work with an iPhone?

Not as the sending device. As of October 2026, textbee says it works only on Android 7.0 or later. Campfire SMS has no app; you receive and reply in your normal SMS inbox on any phone.

Can my agent read my reply with textbee?

Yes. textbee says its MCP server exposes message reading, and its API offers a messages endpoint and webhooks. Campfire SMS returns your reply to the connected agent through its own connector.

What does textbee cost?

As of October 2026, textbee has a free tier of 300 messages a month (50 a day) with no card, and Pro at $14.99 a month for 5,000 messages. Your carrier plan carries the SMS.
