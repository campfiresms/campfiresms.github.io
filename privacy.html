Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Campfire (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and connected-agent SMS messaging service (the “Service”).

Information the Service processes

When you activate or use Campfire SMS, telecommunications providers deliver your originating phone number, message body, message identifier, and related delivery data to Campfire. The Service may process the messages and related metadata needed to associate an SMS conversation with your connected-agent environment, route your instructions, deliver agent updates, prevent abuse, and provide support. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your request.

How we use information

We use information to operate the messaging service, authenticate and route messages, deliver conversational replies and task notifications, suppress duplicate processing, troubleshoot failures, prevent abuse, comply with law, and respond to support requests. Your connected agent remains responsible for the tools, files, network access, and inference services in its own environment.

SMS and mobile information

Campfire does not sell or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. SMS-related information may be disclosed to service providers and subprocessors solely as necessary to operate and support the Campfire messaging service, such as telecommunications providers, hosting providers, and infrastructure vendors, or where disclosure is required by law. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent will not be used or shared for third-party marketing purposes.

How we share information

We may share information with telecommunications providers to receive and send SMS, and with hosting, infrastructure, security, and support providers that process information on our behalf to operate the Service. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a transfer of the Service. This does not include mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or SMS consent for marketing or promotional use.

SMS consent, frequency, and charges

Users separately opt in through our public SMS opt-in disclosure. SMS message frequency varies based on user activity, connected-agent activity, and configured notification behavior. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

SMS transmission and telecommunications providers

SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. Twilio and participating carriers necessarily receive and process phone numbers, message content, and delivery data to transmit messages. Twilio receives the inbound message body delivered from your phone. For agent-originated outbound messages, Campfire normalizes and, when needed, truncates text before sending it to Twilio; Twilio receives the final body Campfire submits, not text Campfire discarded before submission. Under our current provider configuration, Twilio message records may include message content and be available through Twilio’s Console or APIs. Twilio and carriers may retain information under their own settings, policies, and legal obligations.

Data retention and logs

Campfire production application logs are configured not to record SMS message bodies. This application-log setting does not redact content from Twilio records, carriers, connected-agent environments, or sender and recipient devices. We retain other information only as needed to operate, secure, support, and improve the Service, honor opt-outs, and meet legal obligations. Service providers and connected-agent environments may retain information under their own policies. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, protected health information, or other highly sensitive information by SMS.

Your choices

You may stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us. We may need to verify your identity, and some requests may be limited by law.

Security

SMS is not end-to-end encrypted and may be visible to carriers, device users, and anyone with access to your phone. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the effective date above. Material changes will be communicated as required by law.

Contact us

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to campfire.sms.support@gmail.com.
