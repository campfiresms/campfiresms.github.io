Privacy Policy

Effective August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Campfire SMS (“Campfire,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and AI-powered text messaging service (the “Service”).

Information we collect

When you text Campfire, we collect your phone number, the content of messages you send and receive, and related messaging data such as timestamps, delivery status, carrier information, and opt-in or opt-out status. If you email us, we collect your email address and request. We may collect limited website access data, such as IP address, browser type, and device type.

How we use information

We use information to provide the Service; generate and deliver AI responses; maintain conversation context; respond to support requests; prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; troubleshoot and improve reliability; comply with law; and enforce our terms. We do not use your mobile information or text-message consent for third-party marketing.

AI processing

Your messages are processed by automated artificial-intelligence systems to create responses. Do not send highly sensitive information, including passwords, financial account details, government identification numbers, or protected health information. AI responses may be inaccurate.

How we share information

We share information only as needed with vendors that help operate the Service, including messaging carriers and providers, cloud hosting providers, AI service providers, and security or support vendors. These providers process information on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, or transfer of assets.

No sale or marketing transfer of SMS data.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share mobile information, phone numbers, or SMS opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties for their own marketing.

Data retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, meet legal and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary by the information and why it was collected. We may retain opt-out records as necessary to honor your request.

Your choices

You may stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us. We may need to verify your identity, and some requests may be limited by law.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the effective date above. Material changes will be communicated as required by law.

Contact us

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to campfire.sms.support@gmail.com.
