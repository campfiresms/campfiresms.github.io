Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Campfire SMS (“Campfire,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and AI-powered text messaging service (the “Service”).

Information the Service processes

When you text Campfire, Twilio delivers your originating phone number, message body, Twilio message identifier, and related delivery data to our backend. Campfire sends the text of your prompt to OpenAI to generate a response. App-formatted requests may also contain short device and request codes used to match a reply to a request. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your request.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate Twilio webhook requests, generate and send AI responses, associate structured replies with requests, suppress duplicate processing, troubleshoot failures, prevent abuse, comply with law, and respond to support requests. The current SMS service does not build a persistent Campfire conversation history from your messages.

AI and SMS processing

Your prompt is processed by OpenAI’s automated systems. Twilio and participating mobile carriers transmit the messages. SMS is not end-to-end encrypted and may be visible to carriers, device users, and anyone with access to your phone. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, protected health information, or other highly sensitive information. AI responses may be inaccurate.

How we share information

We share information with Twilio to receive and send SMS and with OpenAI to generate the answer you request. Hosting and infrastructure providers may also process operational data on our behalf. These providers maintain their own data-handling and retention practices. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a transfer of the Service.

No sale or marketing transfer of SMS data.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share mobile information, phone numbers, or SMS opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties for their own marketing.

SMS consent, frequency, and charges

Users enroll by texting START to +1 (608) 422-4987 after reviewing our public SMS opt-in disclosure. SMS message frequency varies based on how often you send prompts and use the Service. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

Data retention and logs

The current Campfire backend keeps Twilio message identifiers in temporary process memory to prevent duplicate replies. Successful operational logs include provider message identifiers, outbound identifiers, model name, and status; message bodies and phone numbers are not logged by default. Twilio, OpenAI, carriers, and infrastructure providers may retain data under their own policies. We may retain support correspondence and records needed for security, legal compliance, and honoring opt-outs.

Your choices

You may stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us. We may need to verify your identity, and some requests may be limited by law.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the effective date above. Material changes will be communicated as required by law.

Contact us

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to campfire.sms.support@gmail.com.
