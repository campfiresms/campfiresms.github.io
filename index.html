How it works

  1. Connect your phone

    Verify your number, then connect CampfireSMS to your coding agent.

  2. Start a task

    Give your agent instructions and ask it to use CampfireSMS for updates and questions.

  3. Continue by text

    Read updates and reply with instructions from your phone. No mobile data needed.

Keep your agent running and online. For local agents, keep your computer awake. Native approval prompts still need to be handled in your agent’s environment.
