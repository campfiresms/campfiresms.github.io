HOW IT WORKS

AI that stays within reach.

Campfire is not fully offline. SMS fallback still needs working cellular messaging, the Campfire service, Twilio, and OpenAI.

  1. 01
    Send your prompt

    When internet data is unavailable, send a plain text request to the Campfire number.

  2. 02
    Campfire generates an answer

    The Campfire backend securely validates the request and sends your prompt to OpenAI.

  3. 03
    Get a concise SMS reply

    Response length may be limited for delivery, reliability, or cost. Send another message whenever you need more help.

i
A helpful tool, not a human expert.

Campfire responses are generated by AI and can be incomplete or incorrect. Don’t use Campfire for emergencies or as a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or safety advice.
