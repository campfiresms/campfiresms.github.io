Before you start

Your agent stays on your computer.

Local setup

Windows, macOS, or Linux with a working Codex or Grok Build CLI. The installer requires Node.js 18+ with npx; the local connector requires Python 3.12+.

Keep the computer awake, online, and the agent session active. The local connector cannot wake an arbitrarily idle or stopped session.

What signup asks for

A phone number, its SMS verification code, and a six-digit PIN with confirmation. Verification creates your account; later you sign in with phone + PIN.

Setup includes a package download and a two-way SMS test. Time varies with your connection and existing runtimes; we have not published a measured setup-time claim.

SMS messages

What moves between your agent and your phone.

  • Status updates
  • Clarifications within the agreed task
  • Agent instructions
  • Long-running task monitoring

CampfireSMS carries short messages between your verified phone and an agent already running in Codex or Grok Build. It does not run another model, move your files, or replace native agent approvals.

What a reply can do

Updates, task replies, and approvals.

Informational updates

“Tests passed” or “The build failed.” Learn what changed without checking a dashboard.

Two-way steering

Answer a clarification or ask for the next check within the work you already authorized.

Native approvals

New permissions and consequential actions stay in your agent’s approval flow. A text cannot authorize destructive work or bypass those controls.

How the allowance works

  • Each inbound or outbound Campfire message counts once. A text and its reply use two messages; carrier SMS segments are not separate allowance units.
  • Your account shows the remaining allowance. Setup test messages use messages too.
  • At 40 trial messages or 500 monthly messages, messaging pauses without overage charges. Replies sent while exhausted are not queued for later delivery.
  • The paid allowance currently resets at the start of each UTC calendar month. Unused messages do not roll over. Subscription billing renews monthly.
  • Upgrading keeps your verified phone and existing installations.

Expectations & recovery

If the conversation goes quiet.

How quickly do updates arrive?

Timing includes the agent reaching a work boundary, service processing, and carrier delivery. SMS can be delayed; there is no published delivery-time guarantee or measured reliability percentage. Use it for convenient updates rather than time-critical monitoring.

What if I stop receiving updates?

Check your remaining messages, confirm the computer is awake and online, and return to the agent to confirm its bridge is active. Ask it to send a short test. A stopped local session cannot be restarted just by texting.

What if installation fails or the command expires?

Check Node.js, Python, and your agent CLI first. Create a fresh install command in your account if the old one expired or was used. For an existing valid installation, see the update and recovery steps.

Which countries and carriers work?

Availability depends on the number and carrier. We have not published a verified country-by-country coverage list. Check with support for your country before relying on the service, and complete the two-way setup test.
