About CampfireSMS

A hosted two-way SMS bridge between your phone and your agents.

CampfireSMS operates the messaging service: phone verification, SMS delivery, an authenticated message queue, and routing to your connected agent. The agent provides the model, tools, project access, and task execution.

With Grok Bot webhook wake configured, an incoming text starts a cloud routine that retrieves the message and can reply by SMS while your PC is off. Local agents such as Codex and Grok Build can exchange updates and replies while their session and computer remain available.

Follow the Grok Bot walkthrough or local-agent setup. Plans and message counting are on the homepage.
